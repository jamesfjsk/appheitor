### Barras do dia 1, semente 1

**U1 · am / is / are**
1. My dogs ___ hungry. (is | are) → are
2. The cat ___ tired. (is | are) → is
3. They ___ late. (am | is | are) → are
4. I ___ at school. (am | is | are) → am
5. The lamp ___ new. (am | is | are) → is
6. [My dog] [is] [in the garden] → My dog is in the garden

**U2 · a / an**
1. I have ___ dog. (a | an) → a
2. I have ___ umbrella. (a | an) → an
3. There is ___ lamp in the box. (a | an) → a
4. There is ___ old book in the box. (a | an) → an
5. It is ___ big egg. (a | an) → a
6. [I] [have] [an] [egg] → I have an egg

**U3 · plural com -s**
1. I have two ___. (dog | dogs) → dogs
2. I have one ___. (cat | cats) → cat
3. I see ___ balls. (a | two | one) → two
4. I have ___ cats. (one | two | a) → two
5. There are ___ eggs. (one | three | a) → three
6. [I] [have] [two] [dogs] → I have two dogs

**U4 · there is / there are**
1. There ___ a cat under the bed. (is | are) → is
2. There ___ one book in the box. (is | are) → is
3. There ___ three eggs on the table. (is | are | am) → are
4. There ___ an egg in the fridge. (is | are | am) → is
5. There ___ two balls in my bag. (is | are | am) → are
6. [There is] [a cat] [under the bed] → There is a cat under the bed

**U5 · want to / need to**
1. Qual está certa? (I want pizza. | I want to pizza.) → I want pizza.
2. Qual está certa? (I need to read. | I need read.) → I need to read.
3. I want ___. (water | to water | to) → water
4. I need ___ my homework. (to do | do | doing) → to do
5. We want ___ soccer. (to play | play | playing) → to play
6. [I want] [to play] [soccer] → I want to play soccer

**U6 · can / can't**
1. A bird ___ fly. (can | can't) → can
2. A cow ___ fly. (can | can't) → can't
3. A frog ___ jump. (can | can't | to) → can
4. I can ___ fast. (run | to run | runs) → run
5. A cat ___ climb a tree. (can | can't | to) → can
6. [A bird] [can] [fly] → A bird can fly

**U7 · he plays**
1. I ___ soccer. (play | plays) → play
2. We ___ pizza. (like | likes) → like
3. He ___ fast. (run | runs | running) → runs
4. She ___ soccer. (play | plays | playing) → plays

**U8 · don't / doesn't**
1. I ___ like fish. (don't | doesn't) → don't
2. We ___ watch TV. (don't | doesn't) → don't
3. He doesn't ___ chess. (play | plays | playing) → play
4. Ana doesn't ___ milk. (like | likes | liking) → like
